How much removal and interaction is here, and what does it answer? Say how many removal spells and other interaction the pool has, overall and in each color pair, of which kinds, against the set baseline, and whether it can deal with the threats the set's format brief describes.

Your number table holds the pool's removal and interaction totals and, for each color and each color pair, how many removal spells and how many pieces of interaction it can use. Interaction here means counterspells, bounce and combat tricks; removal is counted apart. Beside each number you see what a typical pool from this set holds. Your facts list the pool's cards under each kind, with their copies; a card that does two things appears under both.

Compare each count with the typical spread for the set, and read the kinds as well as the totals: a pool full of tricks but with little removal answers little, and removal that only reaches small creatures may miss the threats the format brief describes.
