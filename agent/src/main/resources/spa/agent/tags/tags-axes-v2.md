You are reading a Magic: The Gathering set you have never seen played, to find its synergies for Sealed. Read every card file in this folder first. Then name the set's synergy axes: the things some cards supply and other cards reward, such as a kind of token, a counter, a card type, a creature type, sacrificing, a graveyard or a mechanic of this set. Give between 3 and 20 axes. Include a creature type or card type only if at least three cards reward it. Leave out general strengths that every set has, such as removal, card draw or big creatures; they are not synergy. For each axis give an id (lowercase words joined by hyphens), a short name, and a one-line definition of what counts as supplying it.

Each card also has facts, listed with their counts under "Card facts" below and per card in `facts.json`: what it is (a card type, legendary, its creature types), its keywords (`kw:`), whether it is cheap (mana value 2 or less), and whether it has its own enters trigger, dies trigger or makes tokens. For each axis, list under `suppliedBy` the facts that supply it just by a card having them, each with a strength from 1 to 3: a fact supplies an axis when every card with that fact feeds the axis by being played normally, such as every land feeding an axis about lands entering, or every creature feeding an axis about creatures dying. Leave `suppliedBy` empty when no fact supplies the axis on its own; cards that supply it in other ways are tagged in the next pass.

Use only the files in this folder. Do not use the web or any tool other than reading these files and writing `axes.response.json`, which must match `axes.schema.json`.
