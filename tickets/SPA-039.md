# SPA-039: Measure the deck-pick test and tune the adjustment weights

**Goal.** G-004 deliverable 4 and criterion 6: `spa deck-pick` measures how many of the cards strong players registered a ranking picks from their own pools, for a random ranking, set grades alone, and SPA-038's adjusted grades; `spa deck-pick --tune` searches the adjustment weights on `msh` and `hob` only; the best weights are frozen and measured once on `fra`.

## Background the Developer needs

- Read G-004 (deliverable 4, criterion 6, Decided 2, 3 and 6, Architect notes) and the Change notes of SPA-031 (17Lands CSV reading), SPA-037 and SPA-038.
- **Data:** 17Lands public Sealed game data, `game_data_public.<SET>.Sealed.csv.gz`, already downloaded to C:\Users\Josh\AppData\Local\Temp\claude\4def206f97af6599\efe9e1ff-6a68-481a-b5ce-0fb70c6c96d9\scratchpad\g004-data for MSH, HOB, FRA. One row per game; a build is one `(draft_id, build_index)`; read each build once.
- **Which builds** (Architect-derived, fixed; the checks apply in this order, and a build is counted under the first one it fails):
  - `user_game_win_rate_bucket` parses as a number 0.60 or more (Decided 2); a blank bucket is skipped.
  - `main_colors` is exactly two letters of WUBRG.
  - Every `deck_<name>` and `sideboard_<name>` with a count above 0 resolves to a card in the set's Scryfall cache, by full name or by front-face name. A build with any unresolved name is skipped (these are the bonus-sheet reprints the caches lack, G-003 known gap). Report how many builds were skipped for each reason.
- **Candidates** of a build: every deck and sideboard copy that is not a land and whose colours are a subset of `main_colors` (colourless included). Copies count separately.
- **N** = the number of the build's deck copies among its candidates. A build with N = 0 or N equal to the candidate count is skipped.
- **Overlap** of a ranking = (deck copies among the ranking's top N candidates) / N. Ties are broken by expectation: when a tie group straddles position N, it contributes its deck copies times (places left / group size).
- **Rankings:**
  - *random*: overlap = N / (candidate count).
  - *set grade*: by the grade file's score (default `fixtures/grades/<code>.v2.json`), by name or front-face name.
  - *adjusted*: SPA-038's unrounded adjusted grade for pair `main_colors`, computed on the build's pool (all its non-basic deck and sideboard copies, through `PoolParser` or the equivalent `Pool` constructor and `MetricEngine`), with the given weights.
- **Report:** builds used and skipped; mean overlap for each ranking to 4 decimals; the adjusted minus set-grade difference.
- **Tuning** (`--tune`, run on `msh` and `hob` together, never `fra`): every combination of synergy {0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1.0}, curve {0, 0.25, 0.5}, colour {0, 0.25, 0.5}, cap 1.0; pick the combination with the highest mean adjusted overlap over the two sets' builds pooled; ties to the smaller total weight. Write it to `fixtures/weights/tuned.json` with the grid, the two sets and their build counts in a comment field. Then run `spa deck-pick` on `fra` once with `tuned.json` and record the result in the change note.
