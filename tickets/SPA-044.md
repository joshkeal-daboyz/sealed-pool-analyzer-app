# SPA-044: Build the best deck for a given set of colours

**Goal.** G-005 deliverables 2 and 4 (the builder and mana), and criteria 3 and 4. Given a pool and a colour configuration, this ticket builds the 40-card deck the scoring likes best: main colours plus up to two splash colours, for example `WU`, `WU/R` or `WUB`. It works by search, with no model call. Every deck part is pluggable and weighted from a file. SPA-045 adds trying every configuration and ranking them, and SPA-046 tunes the weights.

## Background the Developer needs

- Read G-005: deliverables 2 to 4, criteria 3 and 4, Decided 3 and 5, the Constraints (alterable scoring; every set is different), and the Architect notes. Read SPA-043 (`CardPart`, `Weights`), SPA-048 and SPA-054 (`SynergyTable` with all its terms), and SPA-042 and SPA-049 (fixing tags).
- **Inputs.** The pool, the set's grade file (as `pool-grades` reads it), the synergy table (tags, facts and otags, as `spa synergy-table` builds it), the fixing tags, and a deck weights file.
- **Candidates.** The pool's copies whose `Card.colors` fit inside main plus splash colours, colourless included. Lands are not spells. Pool nonbasic lands are handled under Mana.
  - A **splash card** is one with any splash colour.
  - At most `splash.maxCards` splash cards are played (default 3), each with at most `splash.maxPips` pips of splash colours (default 1).
- **The deck.** S spells and L lands, S + L = 40.
  - L follows Decided 3, with cutoffs from the weights file:
    - 16 when the spells' mean mana value is at or below `lands.lowCurve` (default 2.6);
    - 18 when the deck has 3 or more colours (main plus splash) and the mean is at or above `lands.highCurve` (default 3.4);
    - otherwise 17.
  - Lands are first the pool's nonbasic lands that produce two or more of the deck's colours, or any colour. Then basics fill the rest, split by `LandMath.recommendLands` over the spells' pips.
- **Deck score** is the sum of these parts, each its own `DeckPart` class behind one interface, listed in `DeckParts.ALL`. Each takes its weight and parameters by key from the weights file, and writes its own reason line. Weight 0 turns a part off.
  - **`grades`:** Σ of the spells' set grades.
  - **`curve`:** −w × Σ over mana value buckets 1 to 6 (6 means 6 or more) of max(0, target(b) − count(b)), with targets from the file (default 1:1, 2:5, 3:4, 4:3, 5:2, 6:1).
  - **`creatures`:** −w × max(0, `minCreatures` − creature spells), default `minCreatures` 14.
  - **`removal`:** w × min(removal spells, `cap`), using spells `RulesTagger` tags `REMOVAL`; default cap 6.
  - **`castability`:** −w × Σ over spells of grade × (1 − P(castable on curve)). P is the product over the card's colours c of `Hypergeometric.atLeast(40, sources(c), 6 + max(mv, 1), pips(c))`, where sources(c) = floor(basics of c + nonbasic deck lands producing c + 0.5 × nonland fixing spells supplying c).
  - **`fixing`:** w × Σ over fixing cards in the deck (lands and spells) of max(0, k − 2) × (deck colours it can supply ÷ k), where k is the deck's colour count. It is 0 in a two-colour deck, and worth more as k grows (owner, 2026-11-05).
  - **`synergy`:** w × (Σ over unordered pairs of deck copies of the pair score, the diagonal included for a second copy) ÷ S.
- **Search.**
  1. Start from no spells.
  2. Repeatedly add the candidate copy with the largest score gain, ties broken by collector id, until S spells. S is first 23, then re-derived from the mean mana value; re-add or drop, then repeat until L is stable.
  3. Then repeatedly make the single swap (one deck copy out, one candidate copy in) with the largest score gain, until no swap gains more than 1e-9.

  The result is deterministic for the same inputs.
- **Output.** `spa build` prints:
  - a heading with the colours, the score and each part's value;
  - the spells grouped by mana value, each with its grade and a short reason (its top two synergy partners in the deck by pair score, and its P(castable) when below 0.9);
  - the lands, by name and count;
  - the sideboard: the remaining candidate copies, and off-colour cards counted by colour.

  A pool with too few candidates for 22 spells is one line, exit 1. A bad `--colours` and a bad weights file are one line, exit 1.
- **Alterable** (G-005 Constraints). A test adds a test-only `DeckPart` and sees the score and printout change, with no other code change.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Legal decks** (G-005 criterion 3). A property test over every `fixtures/pools/trk-*` pool, each of the ten pairs, and the splash configurations `WU/R` and `BR/G` checks every built deck has:
   - exactly 40 cards;
   - only pool cards plus basics;
   - no card above its pool quantity;
   - lands L as the rule gives;
   - splash limits respected.
2. **Explained** (G-005 criterion 4). On `fixtures/pools/trk-manabox-01.csv`, `--colours WU`, each part's printed value sums to the printed score (±0.005), every spell line has a reason, and a test checks every line.
3. **Search.** On the same pools and configurations, no single swap of one deck copy for one candidate copy raises the score by more than 1e-9, and the same inputs always give the same deck. Hand-built pools with a known best deck build it:
   - a pool where two cards clearly beat the rest by grade;
   - a pool where a synergy pair beats a slightly higher-graded loner;
   - a pool where castability drops a heavy splash card.
4. **Parts.** Hand-built tests pin each part's formula:
   - curve gaps, and the 6-or-more bucket;
   - the creature minimum;
   - the removal cap;
   - castability on a two-colour deck and a splash card;
   - fixing 0 at two colours, and larger at four than three;
   - synergy with a second copy;
   - the land rule's three cases.
5. **The seam.** The test-only `DeckPart` from Background works. `git diff <G-005 start> -- core/src/main fixtures/golden` lists no file. The gate is green.
