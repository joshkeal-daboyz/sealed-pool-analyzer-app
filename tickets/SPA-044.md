# SPA-044: Build the best deck for a given set of colours

**Goal.** G-005 deliverables 2 and 4 (the builder and mana), and criteria 3 and 4. Given a pool and a colour configuration, this ticket builds the 40-card deck the scoring likes best: main colours plus up to two splash colours, for example `WU`, `WU/R` or `WUB`. It works by search, with no model call. Every deck part is pluggable and weighted from a file. SPA-045 adds trying every configuration and ranking them, and SPA-046 tunes the weights.

- **Quoted from the goal (G-005).**
  - Deliverable 2: "**The deck builder** (search, no model call, as the brief decided on 2026-11-03). For a given set of colours, it picks the 22 to 24 spells with the best total score and fills the rest with lands. The score is the sum of the cards' pool-adjusted grades (G-004's tuned weights), minus penalties for curve gaps and too few creatures, plus a bonus for removal, minus a castability penalty for each card whose colours the mana base supports poorly. The search is a greedy fill followed by local swaps. It is deterministic, and every included card carries a numeric reason."
  - Deliverable 4: "**Mana.** The land count comes from the deck's curve and its number of colours (Decided 3). Basics are split by the deck's coloured mana symbols. The pool's fixing lands that make the deck's colours are played. Castability uses the hypergeometric odds of having each card's colours by its mana value, counting basics and fixing. **Fixing is worth more the more colours a deck needs** (owner, 2026-11-05): in a two-colour deck a fixer is scored on its grade alone; with a splash, three colours or four, it gets a bonus that rises with the colours it supplies that the deck needs."
  - Decided 3: "Land count. Approved default, widened for more colours: 17 lands with 23 spells, 16 with 24 when the deck's mean mana value is at or below a cutoff, and 18 with 22 allowed for decks of three or more colours. The cutoffs are tuned on `msh` and `hob`."
  - Criterion 3: "Every deck the builder emits has exactly 40 cards, only pool cards plus basics, and no card above its pool quantity." Criterion 4: "Each deck's printed score equals the sum of its listed parts, and every card line has a reason."
  - Constraints: "Each scoring rule (a card adjustment such as curve or colour depth; a deck part such as the curve penalty, creature count, removal bonus, castability or fixing bonus; the synergy bonus) is its own class behind one small interface, in its own file, with its own tests. The builder, ranking and printing code see only that interface and a list of parts. Adding, removing or reordering a part touches the list and the part's own file, nothing else. Every weight, threshold and cutoff comes from a versioned JSON file under `fixtures/weights/` (as G-004's `tuned.json` does), selectable with `--weights`, never a constant in code. A part with weight 0 is switched off. Each part writes its own reason line, so a new part explains itself with no change to the printer." Also: "Every set is different (owner, 2026-11-04). Land counts, creature targets, curve shape and how much fixing a set has come from each set's own data, never from fixed numbers that suit one set." So the defaults below are starting values in the file, which SPA-046 tunes.
- **Quoted from earlier tickets.**
  - Keyed weights (SPA-043): "`Weights` is keyed: a missing part is 0, an unknown key is an error naming it"; each card part is "`CardPart` (`key`, `score(card, PairContext, weight): Part`)" in its own file, and a `Part` writes its own `line`, null at value 0. The deck parts follow the same shape with their own interface.
  - Fixing (SPA-042 and SPA-049): the classifier marks a card as fixing when it is a land that makes two or more colours, has basic landcycling or land-type cycling, searches for a basic land, makes Treasure, or is a mana producer of two or more colours; `spa fixing` prints each such card with the colours it can make. Each kind is one `FixingRule` in its own file.
  - Synergy (SPA-048 and SPA-054): `SynergyTable` gives the integer pair score of any two cards of the set, the diagonal included, from the tags, the facts, the otags and the cares; `spa synergy-table` builds it from `--tags` and `--otags` (default `<data>/tags/<code>.json` and `<data>/otags/<code>.json`; `--otags none` reads none).
- **Inputs.** The pool, the set's grade file (as `pool-grades` reads it), the synergy table (tags, facts and otags, as `spa synergy-table` builds it), the fixing tags, and a deck weights file.
- **Candidates.** The pool's copies whose `Card.colors` fit inside main plus splash colours, colourless included. Lands are not spells. Pool nonbasic lands are handled under Mana.
  - A **splash card** is one with any splash colour.
  - At most `splash.maxCards` splash cards are played (default 3), each with at most `splash.maxPips` pips of splash colours (default 1).
- **The deck.** S spells and L lands, S + L = 40.
  - L follows Decided 3, with cutoffs from the weights file:
    - 16 when the spells' mean mana value is at or below `lands.lowCurve` (default 2.6);
    - 18 when the deck has 3 or more colours (main plus splash) and the mean is at or above `lands.highCurve` (default 3.4);
    - otherwise 17.
  - Lands are first the pool's nonbasic lands that produce two or more of the deck's colours, or any colour. Then basics fill the rest, split by `LandMath.recommendLands` over the spells' pips.
- **Deck score** is the sum of these parts, each its own `DeckPart` class behind one interface, listed in `DeckParts.ALL`. Each takes its weight and parameters by key from the weights file, and writes its own reason line. Weight 0 turns a part off.
  - **`grades`:** Σ of the spells' set grades.
  - **`curve`:** −w × Σ over mana value buckets 1 to 6 (6 means 6 or more) of max(0, target(b) − count(b)), with targets from the file (default 1:1, 2:5, 3:4, 4:3, 5:2, 6:1).
  - **`creatures`:** −w × max(0, `minCreatures` − creature spells), default `minCreatures` 14.
  - **`removal`:** w × min(removal spells, `cap`), using spells `RulesTagger` tags `REMOVAL`; default cap 6.
  - **`castability`:** −w × Σ over spells of grade × (1 − P(castable on curve)). P is the product over the card's colours c of `Hypergeometric.atLeast(40, sources(c), 6 + max(mv, 1), pips(c))`, where sources(c) = floor(basics of c + nonbasic deck lands producing c + 0.5 × nonland fixing spells supplying c).
  - **`fixing`:** w × Σ over fixing cards in the deck (lands and spells) of max(0, k − 2) × (deck colours it can supply ÷ k), where k is the deck's colour count. It is 0 in a two-colour deck, and worth more as k grows (owner, 2026-11-05).
  - **`synergy`:** w × (Σ over unordered pairs of deck copies of the pair score, the diagonal included for a second copy) ÷ S.
- **Search.**
  1. Start from no spells.
  2. Repeatedly add the candidate copy with the largest score gain, ties broken by collector id, until S spells. S is first 23, then re-derived from the mean mana value; re-add or drop, then repeat until L is stable.
  3. Then repeatedly make the single swap (one deck copy out, one candidate copy in) with the largest score gain, until no swap gains more than 1e-9.

  The result is deterministic for the same inputs.
- **Output.** `spa build` prints:
  - a heading with the colours, the score and each part's value;
  - the spells grouped by mana value, each with its grade and a short reason (its top two synergy partners in the deck by pair score, and its P(castable) when below 0.9);
  - the lands, by name and count;
  - the sideboard: the remaining candidate copies, and off-colour cards counted by colour.

  A pool with too few candidates for 22 spells is one line, exit 1. A bad `--colours` and a bad weights file are one line, exit 1.

3. **Search.** On the same pools and configurations, no single swap of one deck copy for one candidate copy raises the score by more than 1e-9, and the same inputs always give the same deck. Hand-built pools with a known best deck build it:
   - a pool where two cards clearly beat the rest by grade;
   - a pool where a synergy pair beats a slightly higher-graded loner;
   - a pool where castability drops a heavy splash card.
4. **Parts.** Hand-built tests pin each part's formula:
   - curve gaps, and the 6-or-more bucket;
   - the creature minimum;
   - the removal cap;
   - castability on a two-colour deck and a splash card;
   - fixing 0 at two colours, and larger at four than three;
   - synergy with a second copy;
   - the land rule's three cases.

## Change note

Built `spa.agent.build` (`DeckPart` interface, seven parts in `DeckParts.ALL`, `BuildSetup`, `Deck`, `DeckBuilder`, `DeckSearch`, `DeckWeights`) and `spa build` (`BuildCommand`, `BuildSession` for SPA-045 to reuse per set, `BuildLines` for printing), plus `fixtures/weights/deck-default.json`; the `grades` part uses set grades as the ticket says, not pool-adjusted ones, and no `--grades` option was added. All criteria are covered by tests (agent: parts, search, weights, seam; cli: legality and local optimum over the manifest's `trk` pools and 12 configurations, explained score, printed seam); nothing needs a device, the network or a model.
Two choices for the Architect. (1) The land rule cannot always be met: a pool that can fill only 22 or 23 spells in two colours has no 40 card deck whose land count the rule gives, so the builder raises the land count to what the pool can fill (`BuildSetup.capacity`) and the legality test allows exactly that case; a cycle between land counts (23 spells say 16 lands, 24 say 17) is settled by a search that only allows swaps keeping the rule's count, so criterion 3's "no swap raises the score" holds for swaps that keep the rule's land count and the splash limit (all swaps in every non-cycling case). (2) `.gitignore`'s `build/` pattern ignored the ticket's own path `agent/src/main/kotlin/spa/agent/build/`, so I added `!*/src/**/build/`; the gate forced this edit outside `scope`.
Nonbasic lands that supply two or more deck colours are played first (at most land count minus colour count of them); a card with no grade scores 0; the tag file and Scryfall cache are read only when the `synergy` weight is above 0.
