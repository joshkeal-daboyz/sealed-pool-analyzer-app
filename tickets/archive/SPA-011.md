# SPA-011: Count booster basics correctly in the kit card-count warning

**Goal.** G-001 deliverable 4: "validating the pool against the kit template with a warning, not an error." SPA-005's criterion 6 made the card-count warning ignore basic lands when `basicLandsSupplied` is true. But a Play Booster's Land slot puts a basic in most boosters, so a real or sampled kit holds about 79 to 81 nonbasic cards plus 4 to 6 basics. Every sampled trk fixture is therefore warned as short of 85. This ticket corrects the rule before SPA-010 pins the `analyze` output.

## Definitions

- `kitTotal = boosters * cardsPerBooster + (1 if promo is not null else 0)`. It is 85 for both trk and syn.
- `boosterBasicSlots = boosters * (sum of count over slots whose weights include basicLand)`. It is 6 for both trk and syn.
- When `basicLandsSupplied` is true, the counted number is the pool's nonbasic cards, and the expected range is `[kitTotal - boosterBasicSlots, kitTotal]`, so 79 to 85 for trk and syn. Basic lands in the pool are not counted.
- When `basicLandsSupplied` is false, the counted number is all cards, and the expected range is `[kitTotal, kitTotal]`, as before.
- A `card-count` warning is raised when the counted number is outside the expected range. Its `expected` is that range, its `actual` is the counted number, and its message says which cards were counted.

## Change note

- `KitValidator` now computes `kitTotal`, `boosterBasicSlots` (slots with a basicLand weight above 0) and `expectedCardCount`, all public; the card-count warning uses that range. The message reads "the kit holds 79 to 85" for a range and keeps "the kit holds 85" when the range is one number. `ParseResult.kt` is unchanged.
- `KitValidatorTest`: the old "one card short" case (84 non-basic, now in range) is replaced by the ticket's seven cases; the no-promo assertion moves from 84..84 to 78..84. Rare-count, high-count and empty cases are untouched. The 30-event-basics pool spreads basics over ten printings at three copies each, because 30 of one printing would raise the unchanged high-count warning.
- cli tests updated, as the ticket names: `--brief replaces the set's brief for kit warnings` now uses `trk-text-misspelled-01.txt` (expected 79 to 85 own brief, 66 to 71 with five boosters, actual 76), and `kit warnings write expected as a min and max range` expects min 79.
- Next ticket: any pool export that lists 20+ event basics of one printing still gets a high-count warning; that rule was out of scope here.
