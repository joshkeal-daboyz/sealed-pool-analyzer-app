# SPA-048: Synergy scoring from tags, the pair table and a pool's synergy

**Goal.** G-005 deliverable 7, the scoring half. From a set's tag file (SPA-047), this ticket computes:
- a synergy score for every pair of cards in the set, each card with itself included;
- each pool card's synergy with the rest of the pool in a colour pair, against a typical pool.

It adds `spa synergy-table` and `spa synergy`. The card part is built as a pluggable piece; SPA-043 wires it into `pool-grades`, and SPA-044 into the deck score. The ticket also closes two gaps from SPA-047's Inspector.

## Background the Developer needs

- Read G-005 deliverable 7 (the pair table, a card with itself, a card's synergy in a pool) and the Constraints. Read SPA-047's Change note and `TagFiles.read`. Read SPA-038's `PoolGrader` for how candidates and fitting cards are chosen; reuse the same choice and core's `Card.colors`, never Scryfall's top-level colours (lesson, SPA-039).
- **Strengths.** `E(c, x)` is card c's enabler strength on axis x, and `P(c, x)` its payoff strength; each is 0 when untagged.
- **Pair score**, for cards a and b in the set's tag file:
  - **axis part** = Σ over the set's axes of `E(a,x)·P(b,x) + E(b,x)·P(a,x)`. With a = b this is the same formula, so it gives `2·Σ E(a,x)·P(a,x)`.
  - **partner part**: for a ≠ b, the strength with which a names b plus the strength with which b names a. For a = b, the strength with which a names itself.
  - **score** = axis part + partner part. The table is symmetric.
- **A card's synergy in a pool**, for one colour pair. The candidates are the pool's nonland copies that fit the pair (as `PoolGrader` picks them), N copies in all. For candidate card a with n(a) copies:
  - `syn(a)` = Σ over the other distinct candidates b of `n(b)·score(a,b)`, plus `(n(a) − 1)·score(a,a)`.
  - `q(a)` = the mean of `score(a,b)` over the set's sealed-pool cards b ≠ a that are nonland and fit the pair (each card once).
  - `e(a)` = `q(a) × (N − 1)`.
  - **part** = `weight × clamp((syn − e) / max(e, 1), −1, 1)`, with `weight` read from `fixtures/weights/tag-synergy.json` (or `--weights`). It needs no special case for a card with no tags (ruling, History).
  - The reason line names up to three partners in the pool with the largest `n(b)·score(a,b)`, ties broken by name.
- **Alterable** (G-005 Constraints):
  - The pair score is the sum of two separate score terms behind one interface (`PairTerm`: the axis term and the partner term), so a new term is one file added to the list.
  - The card part implements the pool-grade part interface shape SPA-043 will define. Until then it is a standalone class with `score(pool, pair) → per-card result with parts and reasons`.
  - Nothing is hard-coded beyond the formula; the weight comes from the file.
- **Commands.**
  - `spa synergy-table --set <code> [--min <score>]` prints every pair with a score of at least `--min` (default 1), highest first, ties by the first card's then the second card's collector order. Each line gives the score, the axis part, the partner part and the two cards' names and ids; a card with itself is printed once. The last line gives the pair count, the nonzero pairs, the nonzero diagonal and the maximum.
  - `spa synergy <pool-file> --set <code> --pair <XY> [--weights <file>]` prints one line per distinct candidate, highest part first: part, syn, e, quantity and name. Under each, one reason line names the partners.
  - Missing tag file, unknown set, unreadable pool, bad pair or bad weights file: one line, exit 1.
- **Pinned numbers** come from an independent script (scratchpad `tools/synergy.js`, 2026-11-05) over the committed `fixtures/tags/trk.json`. That script took the candidates and the fitting set cards from `spa pool-grades` output, so its colours are the engine's.
