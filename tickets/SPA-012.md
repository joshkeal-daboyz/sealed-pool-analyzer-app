# SPA-012: Derive the rare-count range from the kit template and exempt basics from high-count

**Goal.** G-001 deliverable 4: "validating the pool against the kit template with a warning, not an error." SPA-005's rare-count warning uses a fixed range of `[boosters - 1, boosters + 3]`, which is 5 to 9 for six boosters. A genuine trk kit always has at least 7 rares and mythics (six rare slots plus the promo), and it has 10 or more 14.1 percent of the time, from wildcard hits. So the rule flags about one real kit in seven, and its lower bound can never be reached. SPA-005's high-count warning (more than 4 copies of one printing) also fires on basic lands, so an export that lists event basics is warned. This ticket fixes both before SPA-010 pins the `analyze` output.

- Bonus-sheet (`sds`) cards are mythic printings, and they count by printed rarity everywhere (SPA-008). A slot's chance of yielding a rare or mythic therefore includes its `bonusSheet` weight.
- `KitWarning.expected` is a range, and the card-count rule from SPA-011 is a model for deriving one from the template.

## Definitions

- For each card position in a kit (each slot's `count` times `boosters`, plus the promo if not null), let `p` be `(rare + mythic + bonusSheet weights) / (sum of that slot's weights)`. For the promo, use its own weights.
- `guaranteedRares` is the number of positions with `p = 1`. That's 7 for trk and syn: six Rare-or-mythic slots and the promo.
- The rare-and-mythic count of a sampled kit is a sum of independent Bernoulli(`p`) trials, one per position. `rareCountMax` is the smallest `k` whose cumulative probability under that exact distribution is at least 0.999. Compute it with exact rational arithmetic, or doubles with a tolerance, rather than by sampling. It is 13 for trk and syn.
- A `rare-count` warning is raised when the pool's rare-plus-mythic count is outside `[guaranteedRares, rareCountMax]`. Its `expected` is that range.
- A `high-count` warning is raised for each printing with more than 4 copies, except basic lands, which never raise it.

## Acceptance criteria

1. For both `fixtures/briefs/trk.json` and `fixtures/briefs/syn.json`, `KitValidator` derives the rare-count range 7 to 13. A unit test also pins the distribution itself for the trk template: P(exactly 7) = 0.24707, P(at most 12) = 0.99889, and P(at most 13) = 0.99987, each within 0.00001. These values were computed by script with exact fractions from the template.
2. Unit tests on the syn template: 7 rares and mythics gives no rare-count warning, 13 gives none, 6 gives one with expected 7 to 13 and actual 6, and 14 gives one with actual 14. A template copy with a single rare slot of weight `{rare: 1}`, one booster, and no promo derives 1 to 1.
3. Unit tests for high-count: 5 copies of a nonbasic printing gives one high-count warning. 30 copies of one basic land printing gives none. 3 copies each of two printings of the same nonbasic card gives none, as before.
4. `FixturePoolsTest` asserts, for every fixture in `fixtures/pools/manifest.json` parsed with its set's kit template, that none of the 26 gets a rare-count warning, that no trk fixture gets a high-count warning, and that the three syn fixtures get exactly these high-count warning counts: `syn-text-01.txt` 9, `syn-text-02.txt` 10, `syn-collector-01.txt` 9. Card-count warnings stay as SPA-011 pinned them. These values were computed by script from the fixture files and the caches. Fixture rare-and-mythic counts range from 7 to 11. The syn warnings are real, because syn's 8-card common sheet puts 5 or 6 copies of a common in a kit.
5. `spa analyze fixtures/pools/trk-manabox-10.csv --set trk` (11 rares and mythics) prints `"kitWarnings": []`. A text pool of 79 nonbasic trk cards plus 30 copies of one basic land prints no high-count warning.
